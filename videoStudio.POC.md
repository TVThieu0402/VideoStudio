# The Approach :airplane:
I utilized the following guidelines on how i approached the design of the project
- **User Stories** - Create the definition of the project. Who are my Users and what do I want them to accomplish
- **Work with a UX/UI Team** - Having a good design resource is key to create the vision
- **Gather my building blocks** - What are the components and APIs available to me to accomplish the project. Do they meet requirements? What are the gaps
